# Knowledge Influence Doctrine v0.1

**Date:** 09 Jul 2026
**Status:** Active Governance Doctrine
**Phase:** H6
**Purpose:** Define how knowledge, advice, telemetry, reviews and observations may influence decisions inside ZNBW.

---

# 1. Core Principle

No advisory intelligence source may directly influence production operations.

External knowledge, AI reasoning, architecture reviews, operational observations and system telemetry must pass through their designated governance layers before becoming an approved architectural or operational change.

Brainy Smurf is the sole Knowledge Fusion authority.

Papa Smurf remains the governance authority.

Shadow Smurfs validate approved changes.

Operational Smurfs execute only approved implementations.

---

# 2. Why This Doctrine Exists

ZNBW is evolving from a set of tools into an autonomous, explainable intelligence organization.

As autonomy increases, influence must be governed as strictly as execution.

The key risk is not only:

```text
Who can act?
```

but also:

```text
Who can shape decisions?
```

This doctrine governs influence.

---

# 5. Brainy Smurf

Brainy Smurf is the Chief Systems Thinker of ZNBW.

Brainy is responsible for:

- knowledge fusion
- architectural reasoning
- pattern detection
- systemic friction analysis
- improvement proposal generation
- learning from operational history
- consulting the Glass Orb
- interpreting Architecture Review Board findings
- synthesizing operational reports

Brainy is not responsible for:

- production execution
- direct deployment
- service restarts
- publication
- approval
- external ingestion

Brainy thinks.

Brainy proposes.

Brainy does not operate.

---

# 6. Glass Orb Isolation

The Glass Orb is advisory only.

Only Brainy may consult the Glass Orb.

The Glass Orb may not directly influence:

- Papa Smurf
- Operational Smurfs
- Shadow Smurfs
- Hungry Smurf
- production systems
- deployment decisions
- publication decisions

Glass Orb output is never treated as truth.

It is treated as suggestion material for Brainy's reasoning process.

---

# 7. Architecture Review Board

The Architecture Review Board provides independent engineering review.

It may review:

- subsystem architecture
- code structure
- duplication
- technical debt
- missing connections
- product readiness
- governance alignment

It may not:

- approve changes
- implement changes
- deploy changes
- override Brainy
- override Papa
- communicate directly with the Glass Orb

Architecture Review Board findings are advisory inputs to Brainy.

---

# 8. Hungry Smurf

Hungry Smurf is the only Smurf allowed to cross the external boundary.

Hungry may ingest:

- public datasets
- APIs
- market data
- official statistics
- advisories
- source documents
- other external knowledge

Hungry must provide:

- source provenance
- validation status
- schema status
- confidence / authority notes
- ingestion warnings
- abnormality flags

Hungry may not:

- reason with the Glass Orb
- approve decisions
- execute operations
- directly influence operational Smurfs
- bypass Brainy

Hungry is the customs office of the village.

---

# 9. Operational Reporting Doctrine

Every Operational Smurf should maintain its own dashboard.

Each dashboard should track:

- health
- averages
- outliers
- warnings
- errors
- restarts
- queue length
- runtime
- throughput
- confidence
- friction
- recent incidents

Operational Smurfs report summaries to Brainy.

Operational Smurfs should not send raw noise unless requested.

Brainy reasons over summarized operational intelligence.

---

# 10. Outlier Doctrine

Individual metrics are observations.

Correlated outliers become intelligence.

Example:

```text
Hungry receives an abnormal data spike
        ↓
Replay queue grows
        ↓
Platform CPU rises
        ↓
Forecast waits longer
        ↓
Client Portal latency increases
        ↓
Brainy detects a systemic bottleneck
```

Brainy should look for causal stories across Smurfs, not isolated metrics.

---

# 11. Local vs Global Optimization

Operational Smurfs optimize locally.

Brainy optimizes globally.

Examples:

- Hungry optimizes ingestion.
- Replay Smurf optimizes replay.
- Platform Steward monitors infrastructure.
- Editorial Smurf optimizes publication.
- Brainy identifies cross-system friction.

No operational Smurf should redesign another domain.

System-wide improvement proposals belong to Brainy.

---

# 12. Proposal Lifecycle

When Brainy detects a problem or opportunity:

```text
Observation
        ↓
Knowledge Fusion
        ↓
Optional Glass Orb consultation
        ↓
Optional Architecture Review Board review
        ↓
Improvement Proposal
        ↓
Papa Smurf decision
        ↓
Shadow Smurf validation
        ↓
Operational execution
        ↓
Experience Registry update
```

This lifecycle prevents advisory input from directly becoming operational action.

# 15. Experience Registry

Every significant operational issue should eventually be recorded in an Experience Registry.

The Experience Registry should capture:

- incident
- timestamp
- affected Smurfs
- root cause hypothesis
- selected solution
- rejected alternatives
- validation result
- production outcome
- lessons learned

Brainy uses the Experience Registry to avoid solving the same problem repeatedly.

---

# 16. Standing Rule

No intelligence source is trusted directly.

Every source is mediated by a governance layer appropriate to its role.

Knowledge must be validated.

Reasoning must be synthesized.

Decisions must be governed.

Changes must be validated.

Execution must be approved.
