# Replay v0.2 Progress v0.1

## Status

Replay v0.2 is progressing from mechanism detection toward taxonomy-aware analogue matching.

## Completed

- Historical case registry expanded to 13 cases.
- Mechanism taxonomy introduced.
- Similarity engine v0.2 made taxonomy-aware.
- Replay quality summary created for dashboard use.
- Analogue summary report created.

## Current Meaning

Replay can now identify exact mechanism matches and related mechanism-family matches.

This means Replay can surface historical analogues even when the mechanism labels are not identical but belong to the same economic family.

## Current Limitations

- Historical case registry remains small.
- Similarity scoring is still simple.
- Mechanism taxonomy is early.
- Results are useful for discovery, not yet client-facing decision support.

## Next Targets

- Expand historical cases to 25.
- Improve explanation output.
- Integrate Replay quality summary into Historian.
- Prepare Papa Dashboard Replay panel.
