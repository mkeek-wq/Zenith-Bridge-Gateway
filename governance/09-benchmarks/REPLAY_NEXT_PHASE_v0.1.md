# Replay Next Phase

## Objective

Transition Replay from:

Historical analogue retrieval

to:

Historical reasoning and self-improvement.

## Priorities

1. Deep case enrichment.
2. Evidence population.
3. Case completeness scoring.
4. Improvement proposal engine.
5. Shadow-Smurf testing.
6. Coding-Smurf work orders.
7. Automatic self-improvement loops.
8. Human governance and approval.

## Long-Term Vision

Replay becomes a self-improving historical reasoning engine that:

- detects gaps,
- proposes improvements,
- validates changes,
- shadow tests updates,
- seeks approval,
- safely evolves itself.
