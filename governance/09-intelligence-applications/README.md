09 - Intelligence Applications

Purpose
-------
This folder contains future intelligence products that reuse the Smurf Intelligence Core.
Each application shares the same architectural principles:

- Ingestion
- Normalisation
- Classification
- Explainable Scoring
- Recommendation
- Decision Support

Current applications

- CareerGPS
  Government and commercial job intelligence, CV matching,
  gap analysis and career coaching.

Future candidates

- Tender Intelligence
- Grant Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Research Intelligence
- Procurement Intelligence
