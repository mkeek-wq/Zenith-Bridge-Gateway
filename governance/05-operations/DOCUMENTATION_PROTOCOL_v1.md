# DOCUMENTATION_PROTOCOL_v1

Status: Active

Purpose:
Ensure governance documentation remains accurate and useful.

---

## Rule 1

Every major subsystem must have a dedicated document.

---

## Rule 2

Every architecture change must update SYSTEM_MAP_v1.

---

## Rule 3

Every new registry must be added to ASSET_INVENTORY_v1.

---

## Rule 4

Governance documents are never deleted.

Deprecated versions are archived.

---

## Rule 5

Technical debt must be recorded before redesign.

---

## Rule 6

Major milestones trigger governance review.

Examples:

* Intelligence Center completion
* CMS Bridge completion
* Client Portal completion
* Replay Engine completion

---

## Rule 7

Documentation should describe:

* Purpose
* Dependencies
* Inputs
* Outputs

before implementation details.
