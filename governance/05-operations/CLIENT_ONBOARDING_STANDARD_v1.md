# CLIENT_ONBOARDING_STANDARD_v1

Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-06-24

Purpose:
Define the governance framework for onboarding, operating and offboarding ZNBW clients.

---

# Objective

Ensure client onboarding is:

- Repeatable
- Secure
- Auditable
- Scalable
- Compatible with future client portal development

---

# Core Principle

Clients consume intelligence.

Clients do not consume internal platform operations.

Internal intelligence construction remains separated from client-facing intelligence delivery.

---

# Client Lifecycle

1. Prospect
2. Trial Client
3. Active Client
4. Suspended Client
5. Offboarded Client

---

# Client Classification

## Trial Client

Purpose:

Early validation and feedback.

Typical Characteristics:

- Limited duration
- Limited support
- Limited customization

Examples:

- Pilot customers
- Beta users
- Demonstration customers

---

## Standard Client

Purpose:

Normal commercial operation.

Characteristics:

- Monthly subscription
- Access to approved intelligence assets
- Access to client-safe transparency features

---

## Strategic Client

Purpose:

High-value relationship.

Characteristics:

- Enhanced support
- Additional customization
- Potential decision-support integration

---

# Client Data Classification

## Public

Examples:

- Published articles
- Public reports
- Public website content

Client Safe:

Yes

---

## Client Safe

Examples:

- Dashboard outputs
- Approved intelligence signals
- Approved transparency metrics
- Approved confidence scores

Client Safe:

Yes

---

## Internal

Examples:

- Workbench packages
- Editorial review notes
- Internal investigations
- Internal governance documentation

Client Safe:

No

---

## Restricted

Examples:

- Raw evidence packages
- Internal registries
- Source archives
- Credentials
- Backups

Client Safe:

No

---

# Client Access Principles

Clients may access:

- Published intelligence
- Approved dashboards
- Approved transparency views
- Approved reports
- Approved exports

Clients may not access:

- Governance repository
- Raw intelligence registries
- Internal workbench packages
- Internal confidence calculations
- Infrastructure services
- Database systems
- Backup systems

---

# Future Client Roles

## Client Reader

Permissions:

- Read intelligence outputs

---

## Client Analyst

Permissions:

- Read intelligence outputs
- Create watchlists
- Save views
- Export approved data

---

## Client Administrator

Permissions:

- Manage client users
- Manage subscriptions
- Manage organization settings

---

# Client Isolation Principles

Each client must be logically isolated from:

- Other clients
- Internal operational assets
- Internal governance assets

Client-specific assets must not be visible to unrelated clients.

---

# Transparency Principles

Transparency is a product feature.

Client transparency may include:

- Confidence score
- Evidence count
- Coverage score
- Data freshness
- Lineage summaries

Client transparency does not include:

- Raw evidence repositories
- Internal analyst notes
- Internal governance records
- Sensitive source materials

---

# Onboarding Procedure

1. Create client record
2. Assign client classification
3. Assign subscription tier
4. Assign user roles
5. Verify access scope
6. Activate client
7. Record onboarding completion

---

# Offboarding Procedure

1. Disable access
2. Remove active sessions
3. Archive client configuration
4. Review retained data
5. Record offboarding completion

---

# Client Transparency Tiers

## Tier 1 — Published Intelligence

Client Access:

- Published articles
- Published reports
- Public intelligence outputs

Purpose:

Provide intelligence consumption without platform complexity.

---

## Tier 2 — Intelligence Transparency

Client Access:

- Confidence score
- Evidence count
- Coverage score
- Data freshness indicators
- Lineage summaries

Purpose:

Allow clients to understand why conclusions were reached.

---

## Tier 3 — Decision Support

Future Capability

Potential Access:

- Scenario analysis
- Replay outputs
- Impact assessments
- Recommended actions
- Opportunity and risk analysis

Purpose:

Translate intelligence into decisions.

Governance Requirement:

Decision-support outputs must remain clearly separated from raw evidence and internal analyst workflows.
