# H7.2 Operational Intelligence Foundation v0.1

**Status:** Architecture Foundation
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** 13 Jul 2026
**Phase:** H7.2
**Purpose:** Define Operational Intelligence as the governed organizational intelligence layer of ZNBW and standardize how the Village explains, measures and continuously improves itself.

---

# 1. Mission

H7.2 formalizes Operational Intelligence as a first-class architectural capability within ZNBW.

Operational Intelligence does not introduce a new governance model.

It does not replace the Village Runtime.

It does not replace the Smurf Engine.

It does not replace the Knowledge Influence Doctrine.

Instead, H7.2 connects these architectural components into one coherent operational model.

The objective is simple:

> The Village should understand itself with the same discipline that it understands the outside world.

# 4. Core Doctrine

Operational Intelligence is the governed composition of standardized operational summaries describing the health, effectiveness and evolution of the Village.

Operational Intelligence exists to support:

- explainability;
- organizational awareness;
- cross-system reasoning;
- continuous improvement;
- governed evolution.

Operational Intelligence is not infrastructure monitoring.

Operational Intelligence is not application telemetry.

Operational Intelligence describes organizational behavior.

---

# 5. Organizational Principles

Operational Intelligence follows several architectural principles.

## Responsibility Before Implementation

Responsibilities define the organization.

Implementations fulfill responsibilities.

Code may change.

Responsibilities remain stable.

---

## Local Before Global

Operational Smurfs optimize their own domains.

Brainy evaluates interactions between domains.

System-wide optimization emerges from coordinated reasoning rather than isolated local improvements.

---

## Explainability Before Automation

Every operational observation should remain explainable.

Operational Intelligence summarizes observations without hiding their origin.

Evidence, lineage and operational history remain available.

---

## Composition Before Duplication

Operational Intelligence composes existing operational summaries.

It does not duplicate subsystem implementation.

Subsystem ownership remains unchanged.

---

The exact implementation may evolve.

- Operational Health
- Current Status
- Freshness

- Outputs
- Consumers
- Dependencies
- Queue Status
- Throughput
- Incidents
- Warnings
- Confidence
- Contribution
- Improvement Opportunities
- Lineage
- Generated Timestamp

Operational summaries communicate organizational intelligence rather than raw implementation details.

# 7. Organizational Responsibilities

The Village organizes itself through responsibilities rather than individual scripts.

The architectural hierarchy becomes:

```text

        │
        ▼
Departments
        ▼
Responsibilities
        ▼
Operational Smurfs
        ▼
Engines
        ▼

        ▼

```

Responsibilities represent stable organizational capabilities.

Engines remain replaceable implementation details.

This separation allows the organization to evolve while preserving architectural clarity.

# 8. Organizational Observability

Operational Intelligence enables the Village to answer questions such as:

- Which responsibilities are healthy?
- Which responsibilities require attention?
- Which organizational areas contribute most?
- Which responsibilities are underutilized?
- Which capabilities overlap?
- Which capabilities should evolve?
- Which responsibilities may eventually retire?

These answers should emerge from governed operational evidence rather than subjective judgment.
