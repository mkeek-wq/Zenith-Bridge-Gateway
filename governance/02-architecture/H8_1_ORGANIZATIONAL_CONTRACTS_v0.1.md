# H8.1 Organizational Contracts v0.1

**Date:** 13 Jul 2026
**Status:** Architectural Constitution
**Phase:** H8.1
**Purpose:** Define the constitutional contracts governing every organizational participant within the ZNBW Village.

---

# 1. Mission

The ZNBW Village is a governed digital organization.

Every organizational participant exists to fulfill a clearly defined responsibility within that organization.

Organizational Contracts establish the constitutional rules that define:

- purpose;
- authority;
- accountability;
- communication;
- governance;
- organizational evolution.

These contracts deliberately separate organizational responsibility from implementation.

Implementations may evolve.

Responsibilities should remain stable.

---

# 2. Why Organizational Contracts Exist

As organizations grow, ambiguity becomes a greater risk than complexity.

Without explicit organizational contracts:

- responsibilities overlap;
- authority becomes unclear;
- duplicated reasoning emerges;
- governance weakens;
- organizational learning fragments.

Organizational Contracts ensure every participant understands:

- why it exists;
- what it owns;
- what it may decide;
- what it may never decide;
- how it communicates;
- how it contributes to organizational learning.

# 5. Organizational Contract Standard

Every organizational participant shall define:

- Mission
- Primary Responsibility
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Consumers
- Producers
- Authority
- Restrictions
- Governance
- Success Metrics

No organizational participant should exist without an explicit Organizational Contract.

---

# 6. Separation of Responsibilities

The Village deliberately separates organizational concerns.

Operational roles operate.

Architectural roles reason.

Implementation roles build.

Validation roles verify.

Governance roles approve.

Learning roles preserve organizational memory.

No participant should simultaneously own conflicting responsibilities.

---

# 7. Authority versus Capability

The Village distinguishes between capability and authority.

Possessing a capability does not grant authority to exercise it.

Examples:

Brainy may reason.

Brainy may recommend.

Brainy may not approve.

Coding Smurf may implement.

Coding Smurf may not redesign organizational architecture.

Papa approves.

Shadow validates.

Operational Smurfs operate.

Authority always remains explicit.

---

# 8. Organizational Communication

Organizational participants communicate through governed Organizational Packages.

Examples include:

- Operational Summary Packages
- Business Intelligence Packages
- Discovery Packages
- Validation Packages
- Change Request Packages
- Experience Packages

Participants consume summarized organizational knowledge rather than directly depending upon numerous low-level implementation artifacts.

---

# 9. External Intelligence

External advisors remain advisory.

Examples include:

- Glass Orb
- Codex
- Future research services

External advisors may:

- provide ideas;
- critique architecture;
- recommend alternatives;
- identify risks.

External advisors may never:

- approve organizational change;
- modify production;
- bypass governance.

---

# 10. Organizational Learning

Every significant organizational decision should strengthen the Experience Registry.

Future architectural reasoning should benefit from historical organizational experience rather than repeatedly solving identical problems.

The Village therefore evolves through governed organizational learning.

---

# 12. Closing Observation

The Organizational Contract is the constitutional foundation of the ZNBW Village.

Individual Smurfs, organizational roles and future digital workers may evolve over time.

Their responsibilities, authority boundaries and governance expectations should remain stable through explicit Organizational Contracts.

The Village therefore grows by improving its organization rather than merely increasing the amount of software it contains.

The objective is not autonomous software.

The objective is a governed digital organization capable of continuously improving itself through evidence, learning and responsible architectural evolution.
