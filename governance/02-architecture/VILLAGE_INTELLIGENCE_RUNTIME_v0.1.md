# Runtime Philosophy

The Village operates as a continuous intelligence organization.

Every Smurf performs a specialized responsibility.

No Smurf operates independently from governance.

Operational intelligence is summarized, reviewed and continuously improves the organization.

The Village continuously:

- observes
- validates
- reasons
- executes
- learns

# Operational Dashboards

Every Operational Smurf should maintain its own operational dashboard.

Typical dashboard information includes:

- operational health
- queue length
- throughput
- incidents
- confidence
- recommendations
- runtime status
- notable observations

Dashboards summarize operational intelligence rather than exposing raw implementation details.

---

# Brainy Runtime

Brainy does not directly monitor every subsystem.

Instead, Brainy receives summarized operational intelligence from the Operational Smurfs.

Brainy is responsible for:

- identifying cross-system patterns
- detecting organizational friction
- generating improvement proposals
- coordinating organizational learning

Brainy remains advisory and has no production authority.

# Runtime Principles

The Village Runtime follows several operational principles.

- Every Smurf owns its operational health.
- Operational dashboards summarize intelligence.
- Brainy reasons from summaries rather than raw telemetry.
- Papa governs material organizational changes.
- Shadow validates approved changes before production.
- Experience continuously improves future decisions.
