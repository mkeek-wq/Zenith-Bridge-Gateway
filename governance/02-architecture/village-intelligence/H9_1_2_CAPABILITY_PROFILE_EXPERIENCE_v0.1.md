# 1. Mission

The Experience capability is responsible for transforming historical observations into reusable organizational memory.

Its purpose is not to explain why events happened, but to record what has repeatedly been observed, how often it has been observed, and how confidence evolves over time.

Experience represents organizational learning.

---

# 2. Architectural Role

Within the Village architecture, Experience acts as the long-term memory layer.

It captures observations from completed work and makes them available for future intelligence engines.

Experience does not create new knowledge.

It preserves learned observations.

# 11. H9 Discovery Outcome

Experience should be regarded as the Village's organizational memory capability.

It stores accumulated observations, preserves confidence history, and enables future organizational reasoning without asserting causal truth.

Experience answers:

"What have we repeatedly observed?"

It intentionally does not answer:

"Why did it happen?"

That responsibility belongs to other organizational capabilities.
