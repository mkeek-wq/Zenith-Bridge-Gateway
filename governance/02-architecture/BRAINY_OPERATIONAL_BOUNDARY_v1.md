# Brainy Operational Boundary v1

## Doctrine

Brainy is not an operational Smurf.

Brainy operates outside the production and operational execution band. Brainy may observe, diagnose, compare, reason, and propose improvements, but operational Smurfs must not depend on Brainy as a runtime source.

## Hard Rule

Operational systems may not call Brainy directly for production or client-facing outputs.

Brainy may influence operations only through:

1. Observation
2. Diagnosis
3. Proposal
4. Papa approval
5. Shadow execution
6. Audit
7. Production promotion

## Glass-Orb-AI Rule

Only Brainy may access Glass-Orb-AI.

Glass-Orb-AI output is never truth. It is suggestion-only and must be filtered through Brainy's auditable synthesis before it can become a proposal.

No operational Smurf may access Glass-Orb-AI directly.

## Operational Dependency Rule

Operational Smurfs must remain:

- deterministic
- auditable
- reproducible
- versioned
- able to run if Brainy is offline
- able to run if Glass-Orb-AI is offline

## Allowed Brainy Role

Brainy may:

- inspect outputs
- identify weaknesses
- compare system state against doctrine
- consult Glass-Orb-AI for suggestions
- produce weighted conclusions
- submit proposals to Papa

## Disallowed Brainy Role

Brainy may not:

- generate client-facing forecasts directly
- act as a production data source
- bypass Papa governance
- write production changes
- promote its own proposals
- act as hidden runtime logic

## Village Model

Operations Village runs the system.

Brainy Hill observes the system.

Glass-Orb Tower suggests possibilities to Brainy only.

Papa Smurf governs what may be tested and promoted.

Shadow Smurfs test safely before production.

## Summary Rule

Brainy may improve the operational system.

Brainy may not be part of the operational system.
