**Date:** 09 Jul 2026
**Status:** Discovery → Integration
**Phase:** H6
**Purpose:** Provide a complete architectural navigation map of the Replay ecosystem, showing how replay intelligence flows through ZNBW, how engines collaborate, where packages are exchanged, current maturity, ownership, and future integration work.

---

# 1. Mission

The Replay ecosystem is the historical reasoning engine of ZNBW.

Its purpose is to answer:

- What happened before?
- Which historical situations resemble the current one?
- Why are they similar?
- What happened afterwards?
- What confidence do we have?
- What can we learn from those cases?

Replay transforms historical experience into explainable decision support.

---

# 2. H6 Objective

Move Replay from a collection of individual engines into a coherent product surface.

The objective is integration, not replacement.

# 4. Architectural Principles

Replay should always be:

- Explainable
- Evidence-based
- Historical
- Auditable
- Deterministic where possible
- Continuously improving
- Never a black box
