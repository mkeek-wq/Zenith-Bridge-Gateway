# H8.2 Organizational Improvement Architecture v0.1

**Date:** 13 Jul 2026
**Status:** Canonical Architecture
**Phase:** H8.2
**Purpose:** Define how the ZNBW Village continuously improves itself through governed organizational reasoning.

---

# 1. Mission

The purpose of the Organizational Improvement Architecture is to describe how observations become governed organizational improvements.

This architecture does not replace existing operational runtimes.

Instead, it composes them into a single organizational improvement lifecycle.

The architecture separates:

- observation;
- operational intelligence;
- architectural reasoning;
- governance;
- implementation;
- validation;
- organizational learning.

---

# 2. Architectural Position

This document forms the bridge between:

- H7 Operational Intelligence
- H8 Organizational Contracts
- Village Intelligence Runtime
- Knowledge Influence Doctrine
- Replay Governance Architecture

It describes how these independent architectural components cooperate to evolve the Village.

# 4. Architectural Responsibilities

## Operational Smurfs

Observe reality.

Own operational execution.

Produce Operational Summary Packages.

---

## Operational Intelligence

Summarizes operational conditions.

Transforms raw telemetry into operational understanding.

Does not recommend architectural change.

---

## Business Intelligence

Combines operational intelligence into organizational understanding.

Produces Business Intelligence Packages for organizational reasoning.

---

## Brainy

Brainy is the Village System Architect.

Brainy:

- reasons;
- synthesizes;
- identifies organizational friction;
- proposes improvements.

Brainy never:

- approves;
- implements;
- deploys;
- modifies production.

---

## Glass Orb

Provides external architectural ideas.

Advisory only.

---

## Codex

Provides repository knowledge.

Identifies dependencies.

Reviews implementation impact.

Advisory only.

---

## Detective Smurf

Discovers external knowledge.

Examples:

- datasets;
- APIs;
- historical events;
- research papers;
- industry practices.

Detective supplies evidence.

Brainy decides whether the evidence is useful.

---

## Papa

Governance authority.

Approves testing.

Approves production.

Owns organizational risk.

---

## Coding Smurf

Implements approved designs.

Owns engineering quality.

Does not redesign architecture.

---

## Shadow Crew

Independently validates implementation.

Produces evidence.

Never approves production.

---

## Experience Registry

Captures organizational learning.

Preserves historical improvement knowledge.

Provides future reasoning input.

---

# 5. Organizational Packages

Participants communicate using governed packages.

Examples include:

- Operational Summary Package
- Business Intelligence Package
- Discovery Package
- Organizational Change Request
- Validation Package
- Experience Package

Packages deliberately separate organizational communication from implementation details.

---

# 6. Organizational Improvement Principles

The Village improves through evidence rather than opinion.

Reasoning remains independent from implementation.

Governance remains independent from reasoning.

Validation remains independent from implementation.

Learning strengthens future organizational decisions.

No participant may independently observe, reason, approve, implement and validate the same organizational change.

---

# 7. Replay as Reference Implementation

The Replay Runtime represents the first complete implementation of the Organizational Improvement Architecture.

Replay demonstrates:

- Brainy diagnosis;
- improvement proposals;
- Papa governance;
- Shadow validation;
- implementation approval;
- organizational learning.

Future organizational domains should follow the same architectural pattern rather than creating independent governance models.

---

# 8. Future Expansion

Additional domains may adopt the Organizational Improvement Architecture, including:

- Platform Operations
- Editorial
- Macro Intelligence
- Forecasting
- Client Intelligence
- Security
- Infrastructure
- Decision Support

Each domain should preserve the same governance lifecycle while remaining operationally independent.

---

# 9. Closing Observation

The Organizational Improvement Architecture defines how the Village evolves.

Operational systems generate observations.

Operational Intelligence summarizes reality.

Business Intelligence composes organizational understanding.

Brainy reasons.

Papa governs.

Coding implements.

Shadow validates.

Experience preserves learning.

The Village therefore evolves through governed organizational improvement rather than autonomous software generation.
