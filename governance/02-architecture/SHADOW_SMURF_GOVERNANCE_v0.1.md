# Shadow Smurf Governance v0.1

## Objective

No automated change reaches production without a shadow run.

## Workflow

Proposal
↓
Papa approval
↓
Operational Smurf
↓
Shadow Run
↓
Audit
↓
Codex Review
↓
Final Go/No-Go
↓
Production
↓
Historian

## Shadow Responsibilities

### Simulate changes
### Detect outliers
### Measure deltas
### Recommend GO or NO-GO

## Example Checks

- Confidence collapse
- Similarity collapse
- Unexpected mechanism explosion
- Duplicate cases
- Broken taxonomy relationships
- Registry corruption

## Principles

- Production remains protected.
- Every operational Smurf can have a Shadow.
- Shadow runs never modify production data.
- Papa retains final authority.

## Long-Term Vision

Support increasingly autonomous system improvements while maintaining governance and explainability.
